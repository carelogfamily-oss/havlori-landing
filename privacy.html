Privacy notice

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This notice covers the Havlori pre-launch website and voluntary signup for early-access and launch emails. Havlori is coming soon for iPhone.

What you choose to share

The “Get Launch Updates” link takes you to a Google Form, where you can choose to provide your email address. We use your email to send Havlori early-access and launch updates. Please do not submit medication details, medical records, or other personal health information about yourself or anyone else.

Google Forms

The signup form is hosted by Google, rather than this website. Information you submit is processed through Google Forms and made available to the Havlori team. Google may also process technical information under its own Privacy Policy. Review the form and Google’s policy before submitting.

This website

This static website does not use advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or cookies. It does not collect your email directly. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as your IP address and request logs, to serve the website.

Your choice and removal requests

Signing up is optional. To stop receiving updates, request deletion of your signup information, or ask a privacy question, email carelogfamily@gmail.com from the address you used to sign up.

About the future app

This notice describes the pre-launch website and email signup. Information about the app’s privacy practices will be provided separately before the app is available.
